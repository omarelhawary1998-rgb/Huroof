# Huroof SKU Catalog — Launch Batch 1 (30 SKUs)

Four collections, ordered by expected conversion. All designs are typeset
(Amiri via HarfBuzz — correct by construction) and generated by
`tools/generate_batch1.py`. Status: **pending native-reader sign-off** before
listing (the standing quality gate). Pricing per `docs/UNIT_ECONOMICS.md`:
digital £7–£12, poster £25–28, mug £16–18, tee £22–25.

Notes from review:
- RL-FAM-001 brief said «أمي أول حب» — typeset as **«أمي حبي الأول»** (natural Arabic for "Mum, my first love").
- Several English lines are deliberate marketing pairings, not literal translations (flagged ◇). Keep them — bilingual designs pair sentiment, not dictionary entries.
- UAE collection is text-only by design: no flag/emblem imagery (national symbol use is regulated in the UAE).
- UAE National Day is 2 December — list the UAE collection by early November for the seasonal spike; Children's and Family lines are evergreen.

## Launch sequencing

1. These 30 do NOT all go to Etsy at once (bulk-similar uploads are an
   enforcement trigger). List per the playbook cadence: 5–10/week, each with
   its own mockups, title, and tags.
2. Website shows the featured 8 only; batch-1 winners get promoted to the
   site as they earn sales.
3. Regenerate or restyle any design with
   `python3 tools/generate_batch1.py OUT [SKU-filter]`.
