# Promotion Plan — "Show Me All The Signs"

Companion to `SONG_REVIEW_SHOW_ME_ALL_THE_SIGNS.md`.

**Honest framing first:** nobody — no AI, no agency — can guarantee virality, and this plan
doesn't rely on personal industry connections. What it does is maximize the probability of
organic spread by (a) making the track technically competitive (done — see the review),
(b) packaging the strongest 15–30 seconds for short-form platforms, and (c) running a
disciplined release process that the platforms' own systems (editorial pitching, algorithmic
playlists, short-form audio pages) are designed to reward.

---

## 1. The asset that matters most: the hook clip

Short-form audio is the #1 driver of viral songs. The loudest, most energetic early moment
of the track is **0:28–0:58** — already cut and exported as
`Show Me All The Signs (Hook Clip 30s).mp3`.

- Use this exact section as the "default clip" when the distributor asks which part of the
  song to feature on TikTok/Reels.
- Every social post should open **inside** the hook, not at the song's intro (the intro takes
  ~25 s to get going — that's a skip on short-form).

## 2. Distribution & pre-release (weeks 1–2)

1. Pick a distributor (DistroKid, TuneCore, or Amuse) and upload the **mastered WAV**.
2. Set the release date **3–4 weeks out** — editorial pitching needs lead time.
3. Claim artist profiles: **Spotify for Artists**, **Apple Music for Artists**, **YouTube
   Official Artist Channel**.
4. Pitch via the Spotify for Artists editorial form **at least 7 days before release**
   (genre, mood, instrumentation, the story behind the song — be specific).
5. Set up a **pre-save link** (distributor-provided or Feature.fm/ToneDen) and use it as the
   only link in all bios.
6. Register the song with a PRO (performing rights organization) and get the ISRC/UPC
   recorded somewhere safe.

## 3. Short-form campaign (weeks 2–6 — this is the viral engine)

- **Cadence:** 3–5 posts/week on TikTok, mirrored to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
  Volume beats polish; only one clip needs to catch.
- **Formats that work for music:**
  - Open-verse / "finish the lyric" challenge using the hook clip
  - "The story behind this line" — talk over the instrumental, cut to the hook
  - Studio/writing behind-the-scenes ("I wrote this when…")
  - Lyric-on-screen loops (the hook clip + kinetic text)
  - Duet/stitch bait: post the a-cappella hook and invite harmonies
- **A concrete trigger for creators:** the title "Show Me All The Signs" maps naturally onto
  *"signs I ignored / signs it was meant to be"* storytime formats — that's a repeatable,
  remixable premise other people can post to your sound.
- Make sure the song's **official audio page** exists on TikTok/Reels from day one (the
  distributor handles delivery; verify the clip it features is 0:28–0:58).

## 4. Playlists & press (weeks 3–8)

- **SubmitHub / Groover / Playlist Push** — budget even $50–150 gets the track in front of
  real curators; target mid-size (5k–50k follower) genre playlists, not mega-lists.
- Find 20–30 independent curator playlists in the song's genre and contact them directly
  (short note + streaming link — never attachments).
- Micro-blogs and YouTube channels that premiere indie tracks (e.g. genre-specific channels)
  respond to a clean one-paragraph pitch + artwork + private stream link.

## 5. Paid amplification (optional, small tests first)

- **TikTok Promote / Spark Ads** on the best-performing organic clip only ($5–10/day tests).
- **Meta ads** driving to the pre-save/smart link with a 15 s vertical video.
- Kill anything with poor retention; double down only on clips that already show organic
  traction. Paid follows organic, never replaces it.

## 6. Release week checklist

- [ ] Post the strongest clip at release-day morning, pinned on all profiles
- [ ] Swap all bio links from pre-save → smart link (Spotify/Apple/YouTube)
- [ ] Ask every early listener to **save + playlist** the track (saves matter more than streams
      for algorithmic pickup)
- [ ] Lyric video live on YouTube; Canvas (8 s loop) live on Spotify
- [ ] Thank-you/reaction content posted 48 h in ("we hit X streams")

## 7. KPIs to watch (first 4 weeks)

| Metric | Healthy signal |
|---|---|
| TikTok clip watch-through | > 60% on a 15 s clip |
| Creations on the official sound | doubling week-over-week |
| Spotify save rate | > 5% of listeners |
| Skip rate before 30 s | < 35% |
| Algorithmic playlist adds (Discover Weekly/Radio) | appearing by week 3–4 |

If the hook clip's watch-through is weak after ~10 posts, try the final-chorus section
(2:35–3:00, the loudest moment of the song) as an alternative clip before concluding the
premise is wrong.
