# Huroof — Launch Guide (digital-first strategy)

Research-backed plan, June 2026. The numbers behind every decision here are in `docs/UNIT_ECONOMICS.md`. The strategy in one line: **earn reviews with digital downloads now, sell profitable prints by Ramadan 2027 (begins 8 Feb 2027), and never list a product below a 28% margin.**

## Milestone 0 — Compliance gate (DONE on the website / repeat on Etsy)

- **No fake reviews, ratings, bestseller badges, or invented stats — anywhere, ever.** Fabricated social proof is a banned practice under the UK DMCC Act (in force since April 2025, CMA fines up to £300k for individuals) and the US FTC rule ($51,744/violation). The website has been cleaned; keep Etsy listings equally clean.
- **Disclose AI assistance** in every applicable listing description, categorised "Designed by" (Etsy Creativity Standards, tightened June 2025). Designs must come from your own original prompts — never bought prompt packs or templates.
- **Arabic accuracy is non-negotiable:** every design checked by a qualified native Arabic reader before listing. Do not list Quranic verses generated by AI — start with Bismillah, single words, names, and proverbs from verified sources.

## Milestone 1 — Open the shop (~30 min + ~$15–29 one-time fee)

1. https://www.etsy.com/sell → shop name "Huroof" (fallbacks: HuroofStore, HuroofArt). List in **GBP** with a GBP bank account (avoids the 2.5% currency fee).
2. Complete ID verification + bank details. Etsy charges a one-time setup fee (~$15–29).
3. First listing = the **digital Bismillah** from `docs/LISTING_KIT.md` (Etsy requires one listing to open).
4. **Opt out of Offsite Ads immediately** (Settings → Offsite Ads). It takes 15% of attributed orders and is optional until you pass $10k/year.

## Milestone 2 — Digital downloads first (week 1–2)

List all 8 designs as digital downloads (£7–£12) plus 2 bundles, per the listing kit. Files: A2-size 300 DPI PDF + JPG + PNG per design. Digital keeps ~80% margin, needs no supplier, and is how the shop accumulates genuine reviews before the season. Target: first 20 real sales/reviews by autumn 2026.

## Milestone 3 — Print partners (week 2–4)

- **Wall art → Prodigi** (UK company, prints in the UK, free Etsy integration, pay-as-you-go): open a free account and pull their pricing CSV — their matte art prints start at £2.59 vs Printful's ~£8 for A3, which is the difference between a 35% and a ~50% poster margin, and may make canvas viable at £36–42.
- **Mugs/tees/phone cases → Printful** (Wolverhampton UK facility, solid Etsy integration).
- **Order a sample of every product before listing it.** Non-negotiable — Arabic print quality must be checked physically.
- Prices per the table in the listing kit. Never list: totes, large framed prints, or canvas under £40 on Printful.
- VAT note: as a non-VAT-registered seller you pay 20% VAT on Printful/Prodigi charges and on Etsy fees, and the margin table already assumes this. Revisit voluntary VAT registration with an accountant only if revenue grows.

## Milestone 4 — Wire the website

`index.html` → `STORE CONFIG` → set `ETSY_SHOP_URL`; optionally `NEWSLETTER_URL` (free MailerLite/Tally form). Push — the site redeploys automatically.

## Milestone 5 — Traffic (start now, compounds slowly)

- **Pinterest is the priority channel** for wall art: planning-driven, high purchase intent, and pins compound over months — exactly the lead time we have before Ramadan 2027. Pin every design + lifestyle mockups weekly.
- Instagram/TikTok: design reveals and process clips (Etsy's CEO explicitly encourages process videos — they're also AI-era trust signals).
- Google Search Console: submit `sitemap.xml`.
- Personalisation listing: add at least one **custom Arabic name print** (made-to-order). Personalisation is the single most consistent success lever among surviving Etsy shops.

## Milestone 6 — The Ramadan 2027 calendar (the real launch)

| Date | Action |
|---|---|
| **By 1 Dec 2026** | All listings live, review-bearing, SEO settled (Etsy search needs weeks to rank new listings) |
| Dec 2026–Jan 2027 | Pinterest/IG push on Ramadan designs; small Etsy Ads test (£1–2/day) on the two best digital sellers |
| **8 Feb 2027** | Ramadan begins — gift searches peak in the final week of Ramadan |
| ~1–9 Mar 2027 | Eid-gift promotion window (conversation data shows gifts dominate the last week) |
| **9–11 Mar 2027** | Eid al-Fitr |
| ~mid-May 2027 | Secondary bump: Eid al-Adha |

Year-round anchors between seasons: Bismillah, Ayatul Kursi (from verified typography only), nikkah/wedding gifts, new-home gifts, custom name art.

## Milestone 7 — Kill/scale gates (end of March 2027)

- **Scale** if: 50+ total sales, all genuine reviews ≥4.8 average, revenue trending ≥£300/month → add designs, raise ad spend, consider Etsy Plus.
- **Pivot** if: sales exist but physical margins disappoint → go digital-only (near-zero cost to maintain).
- **Stop** if: <10 sales despite the season → total loss will be under £100; the assets (site, designs, brand) keep their value.

## Reality check

Median Etsy shop turnover is roughly £900–£1,500/year (computed from Etsy's own SEC filings) and most new shops never build momentum. The plan above exists because the default path fails: it front-loads reviews, refuses loss-making products, rides the one season this niche owns, and caps the downside at pocket money.
