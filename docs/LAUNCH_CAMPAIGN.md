# Huroof — 14-Day Launch Campaign (copy-paste-and-post)

**Goal:** turn views into the first sales, fast. Realistic target for a brand-new
shop: first 1–5 sales in 2–4 weeks, then it compounds once you have a review or two.
No guarantees — but this is the highest-odds plan. No fake reviews, ever (illegal).

## The priority order (where your time pays off most)
1. **Pinterest** — #1 for wall-art / printables. Pins are evergreen and searchable
   for months. Volume + consistency wins. **Spend most effort here.**
2. **Instagram Reels** — short video gets far more reach than photos.
3. **TikTok** — same video, reposted. Cheap extra reach.
4. **Etsy SEO** — being found inside Etsy search (your tags are already set).

---

## The 5 short-term "first sale" levers
1. **Launch discount code** (Payhip + Etsy): `LAUNCH15` = 15% off, first 30 days.
   Put it in every caption — urgency converts browsers.
2. **First reviews, the legal way:** ask real friends/family who genuinely like a
   design to buy it (£8) and leave an honest review. Real buyers only. The first
   2–3 reviews break the "0 reviews" trust barrier that's likely stalling sales now.
3. **Lead with bundles:** the "My First Arabic Words" kids set (7 designs) as one
   £14 bundle is your best gift offer — push it hard.
4. **Seasonal hooks:** Ramadan/Eid and UAE National Day searches spike 4–6 weeks
   early. Post seasonal designs ahead of the date.
5. **Engage, don't just broadcast:** reply to every comment, follow 10 relevant
   accounts/day, save others' pins. Cold accounts get throttled.

---

## The 14-day calendar
Each day = ~15 min. "Pin 5" means 5 Pinterest pins to the matching boards with the
product link in the destination field.

| Day | Pinterest | Instagram / TikTok | Other |
|----|----|----|----|
| 1 | Pin 5 (the religious/calligraphy set) | Reel: slow pan over Bismillah print | Add `LAUNCH15` code on Payhip & Etsy |
| 2 | Pin 5 (geometric + quotes) | Story: "new shop is live" + link sticker | Reply to all comments |
| 3 | Pin 5 (family set) | Reel: 3 designs in a room mockup | Follow 10 Arab-home / Muslim-gift accounts |
| 4 | Pin 5 (kids set) | Post: kids bundle £14 carousel | Email 5 friends the shop link |
| 5 | Pin 5 (UAE + diaspora) | TikTok: scroll through all designs | — |
| 6 | Pin 5 (re-pin top performers) | Reel: "how to print at home in 3 steps" | Check Pinterest analytics: which pin got clicks? |
| 7 | Pin 5 | Story poll: "which design for your wall?" | Rest / plan week 2 |
| 8–14 | Repeat: 5 pins/day, lean into whatever got the most clicks in week 1 | 1 Reel + 1 Story daily | Ask any happy buyer for an honest review |

After day 14: keep pinning 5/day, double down on your top 3 designs, and start one
new seasonal design ahead of the next holiday.
