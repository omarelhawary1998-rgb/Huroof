# Huroof — Unit Economics (verified June 2026)

Sources: Printful public catalog API + official shipping rates (12 Jun 2026), Etsy fee policy cross-verified against multiple 2026 UK guides, Prodigi public product pages. FX £1 ≈ $1.30. Assumes: UK buyer, free shipping baked into retail, seller NOT VAT-registered (so +20% VAT applies to both supplier charges and Etsy fees), no Offsite Ads.

## Etsy fee stack (UK seller)

| Fee | Rate |
|---|---|
| Listing | $0.20 (~£0.15), renews on every sale |
| Transaction | 6.5% of item + shipping |
| Payment processing (UK) | 4% + £0.20 |
| Regulatory operating fee (UK) | 0.48% from 22 June 2026 |
| VAT on all the above | +20% (unless VAT-registered) |
| Offsite Ads | 15% if attributed (optional <$10k/yr — **opt out**); 12% mandatory above |
| One-time shop setup | ~$15–29 |

Rule of thumb: Etsy takes ~12% + £0.40 of every organic sale; ~27% if an Offsite Ad gets credit.

| Product | Why |
|---|---|

| Canvas 18×24" under £50 | COGS ~£41 incl. VAT |
| Framed prints >12×16" | UK shipping jumps to $20.19; 18×24 framed costs ~£60 delivered |

## Levers that change the table

1. **Prodigi for wall art** (UK printer, free Etsy integration): matte art prints from £2.59 vs Printful A3 ~£8.20 — roughly £3–5 more profit per poster and likely makes canvas viable at £36–42. Open a free account and pull their pricing CSV to confirm exact A3/A2/12×16 prices before switching.
2. **Bundles**: a second poster ships for $0.30, second mug $1.25 — multi-item orders roughly triple per-order profit. Always cross-sell sets.
3. **Offsite Ads opt-out** while under $10k/yr: saves 15% of attributed orders.
4. **VAT registration** (voluntary): reclaims the 20% on supplier charges and fees but adds VAT on sales — usually not worth it below the £90k threshold; ask an accountant if revenue grows.
5. **Digital-first**: a £7–12 download nets the same £5–10 as a £25–28 poster, with zero supplier risk — and builds the review base everything else depends on.
