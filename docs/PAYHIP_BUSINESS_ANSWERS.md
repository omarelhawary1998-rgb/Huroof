## "Describe your business / What do you sell?"

```
Huroof حروف is a bilingual Arabic art studio. I design original Arabic
calligraphy, Islamic geometric patterns and Arabic–English phrase posters,
and sell them as:

1) Instant digital downloads (high-resolution PNG print files the customer
   prints themselves, at home or at a print shop), and
2) Premium printed posters, mugs and apparel produced and shipped on
   demand by vetted print-on-demand partners (Prodigi for wall art,
   Printful for mugs and tees).

All designs are original and human-finalised. Design exploration uses AI
tools; every product is reviewed and approved by me, and the Arabic
content is checked by a native Arabic reader before sale.

No physical inventory is held. No fabricated reviews, ratings or
bestseller claims appear in any listing.
```
