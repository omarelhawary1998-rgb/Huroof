# 👋 START HERE — Huroof, the very simple guide

This is the "explain it like I'm five" guide. Little steps. No big words.
Do them in order. You do **not** need to write anything yourself — all the
words and pictures are already made for you.

> 💻 Best done on a **computer**. You *can* do it on a phone, and where the
> phone is different I add a line that starts with **📱 Phone:**.

---

## 0) What are we even doing?

You have **38 pictures** (we call them *designs*). We are going to put them in
**two shops** on the internet so people can buy them:

1. **Payhip** — *your own little shop.* When someone buys here, you keep almost
   all the money. Its address is **payhip.com/HuroofArtDesign**.
2. **Etsy** — *a giant shopping mall.* Lots of shoppers already walk around
   inside it, so it helps people *find* you. It takes a bigger cut of the money.
   Your shop is **etsy.com/shop/HuroofArtDesign**.

For each design you can sell **two things**:
- The **file** (a *digital download* — the buyer prints it themselves). Cheap to
  sell, you keep most of the money.
- A **printed** poster/mug/shirt that a print company makes and posts for you.

We start with the **files**, because they are the easiest and the most money for you.

---

## 1) The four golden rules (read once, never break)

1. ❌ **Never** make up reviews, star ratings, or write "bestseller". It's against
   the law here. Real reviews only.
2. ✅ When a shop asks, tick **"made with AI tools"** and keep the little AI
   sentence we wrote (it's in every description already).
3. 👀 Before a design goes live, show it to **one friend who reads Arabic** to
   check the words look right.
4. 🐢 Put up only **about 5 designs a week**, not all 38 at once. (Etsy gets
   suspicious of shops that dump everything at once.)

---

## 2) Put the pictures on your computer first

1. Find the files I sent you in this chat (the ones ending in `.png`).
2. Make a new folder on your desktop called **`Huroof Designs`**.
3. Put all the design files in it. Done.

> 📱 Phone: save them into your **Files** app (or Photos) so you can find them later.

There are two kinds of picture you'll use:
- The **design file** itself (big `.png`) — this is what the buyer gets.
- A **mockup** (a photo of the design in a frame on a wall) — this is the *first*
  picture shoppers see. They're in the repo folder `products/mockups/`. If you
  don't have them handy, just ask me and I'll send any you need.

## 3) Your FIRST product on Payhip (your own shop)

We'll do the **Bismillah** design first. Open the file
**`docs/listings_master.csv`** (it opens like a spreadsheet — double-click it,
or open it inside Google Sheets). Each row is one product with the title, tags,
description and price already written. We'll copy from the row **HRF-001**.

Now, click by click:

1. Go to **payhip.com** and log in.
2. Click the big **"+ Add new product"** button.
3. Choose **"Digital product"**.
4. Where it says **upload file**, pick `01-bismillah-A2-300dpi.png` from your folder.
5. **Product name** → copy the `title_en` cell: *Bismillah Gold Navy* (or the
   longer Etsy title if you like).
6. **Description** → copy the whole `description` cell from that row and paste it.
7. **Price** → type **10** (that's £10).
8. (Optional) Add a picture: upload the mockup `01-bismillah-framed.jpg` so the
   listing looks pretty.
9. Click **Publish**.
10. Click **"View product"** and copy the web address — that's your buy link. 🎉

You just made your first product. The other 7 "religious" designs work the same
way — same steps, next file, next row in the spreadsheet.

## 4) The SAME product on Etsy

Etsy has a few more boxes, but the words are all in the spreadsheet again.

1. Go to **etsy.com** → log in → click **Shop Manager** (top right).
2. Click **Listings** → **"+ Add a listing"**.
3. **Photos**: upload `01-bismillah-framed.jpg` *first* (the framed mockup), then
   the plain design too. The first photo is the one people see in search.
4. **Title** → copy the `etsy_title` cell from row HRF-001 and paste it.
5. **About this listing**: 
   - "Who made it?" → **I did**.
   - "What is it?" → **A finished product**.
   - "When did you make it?" → **Made to order**.
6. **Type** → choose **Digital** (this lets you attach the file).
7. **Description** → paste the `description` cell. (It already contains the
   little "made with AI tools" sentence — leave it in. ✅ Golden rule #2.)
8. **Tags** → open the `tags_13` cell. It has 13 little tags separated by commas.
   Type each one into a tag box on Etsy.
9. **Price** → **10**. **Quantity** → any number like 999 (digital never runs out).
10. **Digital file** → upload `01-bismillah-A2-300dpi.png`.
11. Click **Publish**. 🎉

> 📱 Phone: use the **Etsy Seller** app — same steps, the buttons are just stacked
> vertically. Uploading the file is easier on a computer, though.

## 5) Turn OFF the money-eating switch on Etsy

Etsy has a setting that quietly takes extra money from small shops. Turn it off:

1. **Shop Manager** → **Settings** → **Offsite Ads**.
2. Switch it **OFF** (you're allowed to while your shop is small).

That's it. Two clicks, more money kept.

## 6) The weekly rhythm (what to do after the first one)

Just repeat steps 3 and 4 for a few designs each week. Good order:

- **Week 1:** the 8 religious/art designs (files `01`–`08`).
- **Week 2:** Family Gifts (the `RL-FAM-…` files). These sell well as presents.
- **Week 3:** the **"My First Arabic Words"** kids set (the 7 `RL-CHI-…` files) —
  sell them as one bundle for £14, plus singly.
- **Week 4:** Diaspora designs (`RL-DIA-…`).
- **Before November:** put up the UAE designs (`RL-UAE-…`) — UAE National Day is
  2 December, so people shop for them in November.

The spreadsheet has a row for **every** design, so you never have to write anything.

## 7) Get free visitors with Pinterest

Pinterest is where people look for wall-art ideas. It's free and it sends shoppers.

1. Make a free account at **pinterest.com** (choose a *business* account).
2. I already made ready-to-post pictures in `products/pins/`.
3. Upload a pin, and in the **"link"** box paste that design's Payhip or Etsy buy link.
4. Do a few each week.
