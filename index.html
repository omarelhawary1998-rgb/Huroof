Arabic script is one of the most beautiful writing systems ever created — yet it is dramatically underrepresented in the global print-on-demand market. English-language stores dominate every platform.

Huroof exists to fill that gap: authentic, AI-generated Arabic art that resonates with 400M+ Arabic speakers worldwide — and with anyone who appreciates the beauty of the script.
